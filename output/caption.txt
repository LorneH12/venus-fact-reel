A day on Venus is longer than its year. 🪐

Venus turns so slowly that one spin on its axis takes about 243 Earth days. One trip around the Sun takes only about 225.

And it spins backwards. If you could see through its thick clouds, you'd watch the Sun rise in the west and set in the east.
